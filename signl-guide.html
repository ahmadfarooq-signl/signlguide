Step 1 of 4

The 3-Pillar
Content Framework

Every strong LinkedIn content system is built on three pillars. Each one serves a different purpose and reaches a different part of your target audience. Use all three every week.

Pillar 1 Educational Content 40% of posts

Share insights, frameworks, and industry breakdowns your ideal client finds genuinely useful. This builds authority. They start thinking of you as the expert before they have ever spoken to you. The goal is to be the most knowledgeable voice in the room without saying it directly.

"3 reasons your agency LinkedIn profile is not generating leads and how to fix each one in under an hour."
Pillar 2 Storytelling Content 35% of posts

Share real experiences, wins, failures, and lessons from your work. This builds trust and relatability. People do not buy from brands, they buy from people they feel they know. Your stories are the fastest way to create that feeling at scale.

"I took a client from zero LinkedIn presence to 3 inbound leads in 30 days. Here is exactly what we did."
Pillar 3 Opinion Content 25% of posts

Take a clear stance on something in your industry. Disagree with a common belief. Challenge the status quo. This builds your distinct voice and attracts the kind of clients who think like you. Polarising content reaches more people than safe content every single time.

"Posting every day on LinkedIn is bad advice. Here is what actually moves the needle for agency founders."
Step 2 of 4

The Post Formula
That Drives Inbound DMs

Every high-performing LinkedIn post uses some version of this five-part structure. Once you see it you cannot unsee it. Adapt it to your voice but do not skip any part.

01
Hook
The first line is everything.

LinkedIn cuts off your post after two lines. The first sentence must create enough curiosity or tension that they tap "see more". Best hooks: a bold claim, a counterintuitive statement, or a specific number. Never start with "I" or "Today I want to share".

02
Context
Set the scene in one or two lines.

Give just enough background so the reader knows what you are talking about. Do not over-explain. One sentence is often enough. The goal is to make the reader feel oriented, not educated. Save the education for the body.

03
Body
Deliver the value.

Short paragraphs. Single-line punches. Numbered lists. White space is your friend on LinkedIn. Dense blocks of text kill engagement. Every line should earn the next. If a line does not add something, cut it.

04
Insight
The one thing they should remember.

After delivering the value, land on a single clear lesson or observation. This is the part that gets shared and saved. Make it quotable. One sentence is ideal. This is the line someone screenshots and sends to a colleague.

05
CTA
One soft next step.

End with a single specific call to action. Not "follow me for more content". Something real: "DM me SYSTEM and I will send you the template" or a genuine question that invites replies. One CTA. Never two.

Step 4 of 4

The Daily Engagement
System

Posting is only half the system. LinkedIn rewards people who engage with others, not just broadcast at them. Here are the four daily actions that warm cold connections and turn content viewers into conversations.

10 STRATEGIC COMMENTS
20 min

Find 10 posts from agency founders or ideal clients and leave a real comment. Add a thought, share a counter-example, ask a follow-up. Not "great post." This puts your name in front of hundreds of the right people every single day.

20 CONNECTION REQUESTS
15 min

Send 20 targeted requests daily with a short personal note. Focus on agency founders, studio owners, creative directors. The note does not need to pitch. Just make it human: "I work with agency founders on LinkedIn growth, would love to connect."

REPLY TO ALL COMMENTS
10 min

Within 2 hours of posting, reply to every comment on your post. This tells the LinkedIn algorithm your post is generating real conversation and boosts its reach significantly. It also builds genuine relationships with future clients.

ADVANCE DM CONVERSATIONS
10 min

Review replies from people you reached out to or who engaged with your content. Move warm conversations forward. Never let a reply go unanswered for more than 24 hours. Speed signals you are serious.

Total daily time: 45 to 60 minutes.
Do this every single day. Not optional. This is what turns your content into conversations and conversations into clients.
The honest part

Why Most People
Never See Results

You now have the full framework. Three pillars. A post formula. A batching system. A daily routine. This is genuinely everything you need. So why do most agency founders who know all of this still not see results?

01
They start strong and taper off.

Week one: five posts. Week two: three posts. Week three: one. Week four: silence. LinkedIn rewards consistency over intensity. One post a week for a year beats five posts a week for a month every single time.

02
They optimise before they have data.

They change strategy after two weeks because "it is not working." Results on LinkedIn compound after 60 to 90 days. Changing strategy at week two is like pulling up seeds to check if they are growing.

03
Client work takes over.

When the agency gets busy, LinkedIn is the first thing to go. But building a content system requires daily execution even when you are head-down on projects. Most founders simply do not have the bandwidth.

04
They cannot stay objective about their own brand.

Writing about yourself and your work when you are the one doing it is genuinely hard. The positioning feels off. The tone never sounds right. You know too much, or you share the wrong things.

This is not a knowledge problem. It is an execution problem.

The framework works. The system is sound. The question is not whether you understand it. It is whether you will execute it every single day for the next 90 days while running a business at the same time.
